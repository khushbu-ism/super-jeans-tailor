About Us

Super Jeans Tailor is a trusted tailoring shop in Nadiad with over 18 years of experience. We specialize in jeans alteration, custom fitting, and repair services with high-quality workmanship and customer satisfaction. Our skilled tailors ensure every stitch is perfect, giving your jeans the ideal fit you've always wanted.

Our Services

Jeans Alteration

Expert alteration services for all jeans types. Slim fit, taper, shorten, or resize - we do it all with precision.

Custom Jeans Stitching

Complete custom jeans made to your measurements. Choose fabric and style for your perfect pair.

Repair & Fitting

Rips, tears, zippers, and button repairs. Perfect fitting adjustments for maximum comfort.

Express Service
